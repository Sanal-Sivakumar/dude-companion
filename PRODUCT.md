# Product

## Register

product

## Users

People who want a small, expressive desktop companion while working on macOS. They use it during long computer sessions for light conversation, reminders, screen-break nudges, system-status awareness, and explicitly approved desktop actions.

## Product Purpose

Create a persistent but non-annoying animated companion that feels physically present on the desktop. It can be dragged and swung, turn continuously, walk with planted feet, sit cross-legged, press itself up after a fall, traverse window tops, climb walls, crawl on the ceiling, chat on demand, surface useful reminders, and perform computer actions only through clear user-controlled permissions. Success means the companion is delightful in short moments, quiet during focus, and trustworthy around system access.

## Brand Personality

Witty, mischievous, polished. The humor should feel dry and situational rather than loud, childish, or relentless. Both selectable characters are clearly adults, stylish, expressive, and original rather than copies of copyrighted superheroes.

## Anti-references

Do not resemble a floating chatbot bubble, a mascot-shaped notification spammer, a static image sliding around the screen, a copyrighted superhero costume, a sexualized dress-up game, or an opaque automation agent that acts without consent.

## Design Principles

1. Feel alive through weight, momentum, posture, articulated limbs, directional volume, and context-aware movement.
2. Earn attention; stay quiet by default and speak only when useful or invited.
3. Ask before acting; permissions are granular, visible, and reversible.
4. Keep personality in the character, not in cluttered interface chrome.
5. Make every joke safe for an always-visible desktop surface.
6. Keep autonomy local-first and harmless; opening, typing, clicking, downloading, and changing system state always require an explicit user request and approval.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.2 AA for all controls and text. Respect reduced-motion preferences, include a quiet mode, provide keyboard access to chat and settings, never rely on color alone, and offer reduced animation intensity. Wardrobe transitions never depict nudity; they use a large comic privacy blur over a neutral anatomy-free surface and a turn-away animation for Dudette.
